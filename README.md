# Cooperative Rural Bank Management System (CRBMS)

Welcome to the Cooperative Rural Bank Management System (CRBMS)! This system is designed to modernize and digitalize the operations of a Cooperative Rural Bank (CRB).

### 🌟 Project Vision
Our goal is to transition from manual, paper-based operations to a secure, fast, and reliable digital system. This MVP (Minimum Viable Product) focuses on essential banking features, ensuring a smooth transition for staff and better service for our members.

### 🎯 Key Objectives
- **Digitalize Operations:** Replace manual registers with a unified digital platform.
- **Improve Accuracy:** Reduce calculation and accounting errors.
- **Instant Reporting:** Generate financial reports instantly without manual tallying.
- **Enhance Customer Service:** Provide faster and more efficient service to members.
- **Secure Records:** Keep customer data and transaction history safe and backed up.
- **Future-Ready:** Prepare the society for upcoming online and mobile banking features.

### 👥 Who Uses This System?
The system is built with different roles to ensure security and proper management:
- **Administrator:** Manages the entire system and users.
- **Bank Manager:** Approves loans, oversees operations, and reviews reports.
- **Cashier:** Handles day-to-day deposits and withdrawals.
- **Loan Officer:** Processes loan applications and manages recoveries.
- **Accountant:** Manages the cash book and generates financial reports.
- **Auditor:** Has read-only access for auditing and compliance.

### 💻 Core Features (MVP)
- **Dashboard:** A central hub showing daily summaries (deposits, withdrawals, balances, etc.) and visual charts.
- **Member Management:** Securely store and manage member profiles, including personal details, photos, and signatures.
- **Savings Module:** Open accounts, process deposits/withdrawals, check balances, and print passbooks.
- **Loan Management:** Manage the entire loan lifecycle—from application and manager approval to disbursement and monthly recovery.
- **Cash Book:** Track daily opening balances, cash-in, cash-out, and closing balances.
- **Reporting:** Instantly generate critical reports like Daily Cash, Savings, Loans, Trial Balance, and Income Statements.
- **Notifications & Search:** Quickly find member or account details and receive alerts for overdue loans or upcoming installments.

### 🚀 Future Enhancements (Phase 2)
Once the foundation is solid, we plan to introduce:
- SMS Notifications for transactions.
- An Online Member Portal and Mobile App.
- Digital Signatures and Biometric Login.
- Online Payment Gateways and ATM Integration.
